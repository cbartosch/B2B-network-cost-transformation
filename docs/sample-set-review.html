Twenty-six outside-in estimates

What the set says, where each number comes from, and how much of it now rests on observed prices rather than seeded assumptions.

The cost side has its first observed evidence, and it is thin but real. Twenty-six companies price without error and every gate passes. Ten researched market prices now reach the estimates; 10 of 26 companies carry grade C value, and Boots rests 85% on it.

Across the set, value-weighted, that is 12.4% grade C against 87.6% grade E — an expert assumption with no transaction, quote or published benchmark behind it. Sixteen companies remain entirely seeded.

Research determines how many sites; the rate card determines what they cost. What changed is that ten cells of that card are now observed rather than assumed.

What is actually evidenced

Two questions, often conflated: how many sites, and what each one costs.

  • Quantities are part-evidenced. Site counts start from a declared footprint and research supersedes where it finds a sourced count at least as large — 46 rows changed out of 1,012 findings, a 4.5% promotion yield. The declared base is ANALYST_ENTERED_SCOPE, so evidenced_share stays 0: it measures the origin of a quantity, and that origin is a specification rather than a discovery.
  • Unit costs are 12.4% evidenced, value-weighted. Ten grade C researched prices against a card otherwise grade E: “indicative starting position set at seed time; no transaction, quote or published benchmark behind it.”
  • So 19.2% savings still rests mostly on assumption — less so than before, and not yet a claim about what these companies spend.

Numbers I introduced that move money and are not sourced

Net effect on the set +2.8%. Each is defensible in direction and invented in magnitude.

24 density cost multipliers

93% of L0 spend prices through one. The direction is tested — cost follows the local loop, Ethernet rural ×2.00 against mobile ×1.05 — and the magnitudes have nothing behind them.

Four authored sector shapes

forecourt-network, clinic-network, merchant-network, agency-network. Shares invented; the orderings they encode are the defensible part. agency-network assumes a tied-agent insurer, clinic-network makes a healthcare estate 78% small sites.

The metric that could not see its own subject

Ten grade C prices went live and unsourced_price_share stayed at exactly 100%. The cause was getattr(prior, "evidence_grade", None) applied to a value match_prior returns as a dict — so it took the default every time, and every row reported grade E whatever priced it. The comment above that line describes the fix for audit finding A-02, that a seeded guess and a cleared benchmark contributed identically to confidence. The code never implemented it, and every figure in the Grade C column above was invisible until it was corrected.

89 interpolated rate-card rows

The 1–10 Gbps gap, filled geometrically from each country’s own anchors. The method is checkable — it reproduces tiers the card already holds to within 0.7% — but it remains grade E derived from grade E.

Scope, and what the flags mean

  • All 26 are PARTIAL_COUNTRIES and the denominator is DECLARED_UNKNOWN. This is not “three of twenty modelled”, it is “three modelled, total unknown”.
  • Three are PARTIAL at v0: Lufthansa, Nestlé, Rio Tinto.
  • One plausibility flag. UPS reads HIGH against a grade F tripwire that only catches order-of-magnitude error — a prompt to look, not a finding.
  • Five are short of 17 domains: Deutsche Bahn and Accor at 14, Fresenius at 15, Inditex and Rio Tinto at 16.

The recurring defect

Nearly everything found in this set was a guard that existed and checked the weaker property. set_industry_if_absent asked whether an industry code was a code, never this entity’s. The footprint merge could not tell “not listed” from “excluded”. Promotion let write order choose between a national total and a state breakdown. The rate card let query order choose between a researched price and the seed it displaced. The evidence metric read a dict with getattr and reported a constant.

In every case the output looked normal, which is why reading the code found none of them and running it found all of them — usually as an expected movement that did not happen.

Before any of this leaves the room

  • Sixteen companies carry no observed price at all. The ten that do are those with GB, ES, NL or US circuits at a researched tier. Every other market is still entirely seeded.
  • Ten prices is not a rate card. The evidence is real and narrow — four countries, four products, single observations each, widened to the card’s own ±25% rather than to anything measured.
  • CR-03 plausibility ranges, CR-05 wave parameters, CR-01 coverage thresholds still need values.
  • Nothing here is safe for external use on the strength of the gate alone. A clean gate is necessary and not sufficient, and the tooling says so on every run.
